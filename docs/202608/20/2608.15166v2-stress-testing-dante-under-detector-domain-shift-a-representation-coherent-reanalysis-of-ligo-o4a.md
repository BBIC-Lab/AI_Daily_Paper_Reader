## 摘要
这是域自适应瞬态评估网络（DANTE）预印本的第六版，它在表示失配和观测运行期自适应条件下对该无监督瞬态噪声流程进行压力测试。我们使用冻结的 DINOv2 图像块嵌入和 Top-k 多实例评分，重新分析了来自 42 个 LIGO O4a 时段的 10,429 个探测器时间应变候选事件。候选事件和本地背景 Q 变换的 Q 值均在 [4,64] 范围内，探测器特定阈值通过时间块自助法从 5,000 个运行本地窗口中校准。一致性分析得出 6,365 个 ROBUST、1,275 个 AMBIGUOUS 和 2,789 个 BACKGROUND 统计判定；在 10,372 个配对的历史判定中，有 4,676 个与跨表示的 v5 分析不同。直接对照解决了 H1 在本地自适应后的 O3b-O4a 分数偏移和降低问题，但未解决 L1 的问题；而已知毛刺的分离取决于探测器和形态。重复研究量化了对背景抽样、聚类种子、字典大小和白化的总体依赖性敏感度，证明了本地索引吸收，并识别出条件性低 Q 盲区。一个保守的 H1-L1 最大偏移筛选得到 8,806 个值中有 13 个超过阈值，但其在源值和按事件合并的零分布最大值不可交换。主要双零假设 PEM 终点未显示可分辨的 ROBUST-BACKGROUND 富集（p=1.000），两个目录重叠与循环位移覆盖代理一致（p=0.651）。纯模拟致密双星对照显示，新颖性、本地判定和物理符合性之间存在依赖于探测器和距离的不一致。我们撤回 v5 版本的发现、速率限制、目录召回和巡天范围稳定性解释。所支持的结果是一组针对无监督探测器表征的已测量失效模式和有效性条件，而不是新的毛刺类别或天体物理搜索。

## Abstract
This sixth version of the Domain-Adaptive Network for Transient Evaluation (DANTE) preprint stress-tests an unsupervised transient-noise pipeline under representation mismatch and observing-run adaptation. We reanalyse 10,429 detector-time strain candidates from 42 LIGO O4a sessions using frozen DINOv2 patch embeddings and a Top-k multiple-instance score. Candidate and native-background Q-transforms share Q in [4,64], and detector-specific thresholds are calibrated from 5,000 run-native windows by temporal-block bootstrap. The coherent analysis yields 6,365 ROBUST, 1,275 AMBIGUOUS, and 2,789 BACKGROUND statistical dispositions; 4,676 of 10,372 paired historical dispositions differ from the cross-representation v5 analysis. Direct controls resolve an O3b-O4a score shift and reduction after native adaptation for H1, but not L1, while known-glitch separation is detector- and morphology-dependent. Replicated studies quantify population-dependent sensitivity to background draw, clustering seed, dictionary size, and whitening, demonstrate native-index absorption, and identify conditional low-Q blindness. A conservative H1-L1 max-shift screen yields 13/8,806 values above threshold, but its on-source values and pooled per-event null maxima are not exchangeable. The primary two-null PEM endpoint shows no resolved ROBUST-BACKGROUND enrichment (p=1.000), and two catalogue overlaps are consistent with a circular-shift coverage proxy (p=0.651). Simulation-only compact-binary controls show detector- and distance-dependent disagreement between novelty, native disposition, and physical coincidence. We withdraw the v5 discovery, rate-limit, catalogue-recall, and survey-wide stability interpretations. The supported result is a measured set of failure modes and validity conditions for unsupervised detector characterization, not a new glitch class or an astrophysical search.
