## 摘要
使用4D-STEM的纳米衍射已成为材料研究中定量纳米尺度结构成像的关键技术，但在结构复杂材料中解读其高维数据集仍是一个主要瓶颈。现有的分析流程通常依赖结构模型、人工标注、预定义类别或样本特定的启发式方法，限制了它们表征异质复杂材料的能力。在这里，我们介绍DINO4DSTEM，一种自监督机器学习框架，可直接从原始衍射数据中自动发现具有结构意义的状态。无需结构模型、人工标签、预定义类别数或系统特定的参数调整，该框架学习表示，根据衍射图案的内在结构相似性对其进行组织，将大量低剂量测量结果转化为定量纳米尺度结构图。在多种数据集上，DINO4DSTEM一致地识别出主要的结构自由度，提供无需人工监督的分割。我们将该框架应用于吲哚美辛的结晶过程，吲哚美辛是一种对电子束敏感的多畴药物体系，揭示了结晶性从部分有序的前驱体中产生，并跨越连续的结构有序谱。所发现的纳米尺度结构状态被映射，以定量揭示整个样品中有序性的演化。通过用通用的自监督表示学习取代任务特定分析，DINO4DSTEM为复杂材料中的定量纳米尺度结构成像提供了一个广泛适用的框架，使得在异质、电子束敏感体系中发现涌现的结构组织成为可能。

## Abstract
Nanodiffraction using 4D-STEM has become a key technique for quantitative nanoscale structural mapping in materials research, yet interpreting its high-dimensional datasets in structurally complex materials remains a major bottleneck. Existing analysis workflows typically rely on structural models, manual annotation, predefined classes, or sample-specific heuristics, limiting their ability to characterize heterogeneous complex materials. Here, we introduce DINO4DSTEM, a self-supervised machine learning framework that automatically discovers structurally meaningful states directly from raw diffraction data. Without structural models, manual labels, a predefined number of classes, or system-specific parameter tuning, the framework learns representations that organize diffraction patterns by their intrinsic structural similarities, transforming large collections of low-dose measurements into quantitative nanoscale structure maps. Across diverse datasets, DINO4DSTEM consistently identifies the dominant structural degrees of freedom, providing segmentation without human supervision. We applied the framework to the crystallization of indomethacin, a beam-sensitive, multidomain pharmaceutical system, revealing that crystallinity emerges from a partially ordered precursor and spans a continuous spectrum of structural order. The discovered nanoscale structural states are mapped to reveal the evolution of order across the specimen quantitatively. By replacing task-specific analysis with general self-supervised representation learning, DINO4DSTEM provides a broadly applicable framework for quantitative nanoscale structural mapping in complex materials, enabling the discovery of emergent structural organization in heterogeneous, beam-sensitive systems.

## 7. 优点

- **无需人工干预**：不依赖结构模型、人工标注、预定义类别数或样本特定调参，显著降低使用门槛。

- **物理可解释性**：径向散射损失将类别与方位角平均散射剖面绑定，学习到的类别可对应明确的衍射特征；类平均衍射还可用于后续晶体学分析。
